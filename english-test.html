Read the short text and choose the correct answer from A, B, C or D.

Anna and her husband live in Scotland. Many years ago, they travelled to London for a holiday. They went there by train and visited several famous places, including museums, historic buildings and the River Thames.

Anna wanted to go shopping, so she visited a large department store. Her husband did not want to go inside. He preferred reading, so he found a quiet place outside and read his book while Anna went shopping.

The store had many interesting products, but they were very expensive. Anna looked around but decided not to buy anything. While she was there, she unexpectedly met two friends from school. They talked about their school days and later had tea together.

She chose a pastry, and her friends had scones with cream and jam. They talked for a long time. When she finally left the store, she remembered that her husband was still waiting outside.

She checked the time and hurried back to the bench. Her husband was still reading. He looked up, smiled and joked that she had returned quickly.
